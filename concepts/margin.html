the models

sprout 523m · oct 2026

12 billion tokens from a four source mix, with the optimizer state sharded across 8 tpu chips. then chat finetuned on all of smol-smoltalk, twice. you can talk to it in your browser.

once wasn't enough. the first run still rambled

lilbase 297m · sep 2026

a llama style base model, pretrained from scratch in jax on a free kaggle tpu. it beats gpt-2 small on hellaswag, 36.8 to 35.1, and on arc-easy, 48.1 to 40.6. it loses on lambada.

kaggle gave me 1 working chip out of 8 once. the script now refuses to start

lilchat 297m · sep 2026

lilbase taught to chat in one night on my macbook air, in mlx, with the loss only on the assistant turns. val loss went from 2.230 to 1.337. it told me the capital of australia is melbourne.

then perth. it's canberra
