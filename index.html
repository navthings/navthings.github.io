    research, sep 2026

    the effect of corpus size on the performance of a llm. the same 8.2m model trained on 1, 5, 10 and 15% of tinystories, nothing else changed.

    what went wrong: 15% did slightly worse than 10%. it probably never got through all its data in 5000 steps.
