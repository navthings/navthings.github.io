chaosmaker

a tiny unix machine running right here in your browser. its built on termos, a whole little os written in typescript by chaosmaker. type help to start.

up and down for history, tab to complete. files you make go away when you leave.

thanks to ChaosMaker for termos, and for letting me build something on it. its apache 2.0, the source is on their gitea.
