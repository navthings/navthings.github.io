why

everything before this was trained on my laptop. tinystories is great for learning, but its simple little stories. i wanted a proper base model, one thats actually read a decent chunk of the internet.

my macbook air was never gonna do that. kaggle gives you free time on a tpu v5e-8, which is 8 of googles ai chips, so i rewrote the whole thing in jax and moved it there.

the model

its llama style, same as everything since lilstory, just a lot bigger. it read 6.1 billion tokens of fineweb-edu, which is about the right amount for its size. thats the chinchilla thing, around 20 tokens per param.

how it ran

each step is 524,288 tokens, split evenly over the 8 chips. so every chip chews through 65,536 tokens, then they all agree on one update.

one step, 8 chips65,536 tokens each

the catch is a kaggle session only lasts about 8.4 hours. so the script saves a checkpoint and stops before its time runs out, and the next session picks up from there.

what went wrong

kaggle sometimes gives you a broken tpu with only 1 chip working instead of 8. so now the script just refuses to run unless it sees all 8.

i also tried doubling the batch size. it ran out of memory by like 400mb. so close.

how it did

it beats gpt-2 small on hellaswag and arc-easy and loses on lambada. to be fair gpt-2 is from 2019, trained on different data, and lilbase is more than twice its size, so its not exactly a fair fight lol.

its a base model, so it just continues whatever you give it instead of answering. it gets grammar right and makes up most facts.
