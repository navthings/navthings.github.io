i redid my whole website

i looked at my website and it said "machine learning engineer and researcher" at the top. thats not really me anymore. i still like the models i made, but lately i build random stuff, im captain of a hack club team and im starting a youtube channel. the site was showing the september version of me, so i redid all of it.

the inspo

two sites. tongyu.fish is my favourite. a big name, a few lines, and an ascii shark that follows your mouse. it has so much personality and barely anything on it. and gabin.gay, which has hand drawn flowers you can throw around the page. both feel like a person made them, not a template.

the thing on the home page

i wanted one ascii toy like tongyus shark. first idea was my rocket, then a utah teapot that poured tea when you clicked it, which was funny but kinda mid. now its my @n logo, made 3d and drawn in ascii like donut.c. it looks at your cursor, the light follows your cursor, and you can grab it and fling it. it spins and then settles back facing you. theres a spin counter if you want to waste time.

under the hood the logo gets turned into a distance field once when the page loads, then every character on the left side of the screen is a ray that checks if it hits the logo. the motion is all springs, which is why it overshoots a tiny bit and feels alive instead of robotic.

claude helped me write a lot of the code for this. the taste is mine though, i made it redo the home like five times.
