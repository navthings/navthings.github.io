lilstory: 8m parameters of tiny stories
2026-09-04

lilstory is the first one that's an actual language model. 8.2 million parameters, llama-style, trained from scratch on tinystories, a dataset of short simple stories made for small models like this.

what changed from the transformer toys:

a real tokenizer. byte-pair encoding learns common chunks of words from the data. mine has 8,000 of them.
a real architecture. the llama model from hugging face transformers, with 4 layers, 256 wide, 4 attention heads and a context of 384 tokens.
a real training loop. adamw at a learning rate of 0.001, batches of 32, on my macbook's gpu.

it writes little stories that mostly make sense, then wander off. it was also the first thing i put on ollama, so anyone can run it with one command:

ollama run navthings/lilstory

i also used it for a small experiment on how much training data matters, which turned into my first paper. that's the next post.
