a transformer that only knows one sentence
2026-08-30

this was the first ml thing i made. you type a sentence, pick a word from it, and it guesses the word that comes next.

the catch is that your sentence is the whole training set. it splits the sentence on spaces, gives every word a number, and trains on every "words so far, then the next word" pair in it. so it isn't really predicting anything, it's memorising. but all the parts of a real transformer are in there: an embedding that turns words into numbers, attention, a layer that turns the result back into a guess over the words it knows, and a loss that says how wrong the guess was.

it's tiny. each word is 8 numbers, there are 2 attention heads and a single layer, all in pytorch.

python3 mdl.py

the point was to see the whole loop run with nothing hidden. once i'd watched the loss drop on a sentence i typed myself, the big models stopped feeling like magic.
