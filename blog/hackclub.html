hack club took over my life

i joined hack club this year and its kind of taken over my life. its a community of teenagers who make stuff, and they run these programs where you build a thing, ship it, and they send you something back. the one im in right now is fallen. you build hardware, log at least 90 hours of work in a journal, and the people who finish get to show their projects in shenzhen in december.

team sami

fallen splits everyone into teams, and i ended up on team sami. now im the captain somehow. most of what that means is hanging out in the slack channel, getting people to join, and helping with their builds when they get stuck. someone had a dfplayer audio module that wouldnt light up on an esp32, and we went through the wiring together until we found the problem. we got the team to 998 members at one point and i was spamming the channel trying to break 1k.

what i built

i started with lilrps, a rock paper scissors robot that reads your hand before you finish throwing. i got bored of it pretty fast. then i did liltvc, a rocket that balances itself on a stand with a gimbal i made in fusion, a controller pcb i made in kicad, and a pid sim you can grab and flick with your mouse. that one was really fun but im not a rocket guy.

now its dice of god. its a dice that rolls using radioactive decay. a geiger tube clicks whenever background radiation hits it, and the exact microsecond of each click becomes the random number. the board makes about 380 volts from usb, which was scary to design. its submitted, and im waiting on the grant before i can order the tube.

i also did the 8-pin grant for lilalt, a tiny altimeter board with an attiny412 that beeps out your rockets max height after it lands.

the pcbs

i had never made a pcb before fallen. now ive done three in kicad. every board has the names of people from the fallen slack on the back in silkscreen, because they helped and its cool to have them on there.

why its good

the deadline makes you finish stuff, and the journal makes you write down what you did, which i never did before. plus everyone in there is building something weird at the same time. if youre a teenager and you like making things, join. and pick team sami.
